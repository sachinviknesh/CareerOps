# 15. OFFLINE RESUME QUALITY ANALYSIS



Create:



```text

GET /api/candidates/me/resume-analysis

```



This must work without AI.



It should inspect extracted resume text and provide useful checks.



Check for:



### Section presence



Detect common sections such as:



```text

Summary/Profile

Education

Experience

Projects

Skills

Certifications

Achievements

```



Do not require every section.



Report what exists and what is missing.



---



### Text extraction quality



If the document extracted almost no text:



```text

Possible image-only or extraction-poor resume.

```



Do not claim certainty.



---



### Experience structure



Check whether experience/project entries appear to be present.



---



### Quantified evidence



Detect the presence of numbers/metrics in project or experience bullets.



Example:



```text

20%

30 users

2 seconds

50 requests

```



Do not invent metrics.



---



### Technical terminology



Compare technologies found in the resume against the selected opportunity's description.



---



### Job-title alignment



Compare the candidate's stated roles and previous role titles against the target opportunity title.



---



### Resume readability



Check whether the extracted text contains a reasonable amount of meaningful content.



Return explanatory findings instead of pretending this is an official ATS score.
