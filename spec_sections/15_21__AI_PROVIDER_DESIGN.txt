# 21. AI PROVIDER DESIGN



Do NOT tightly couple the project to one AI company.



Use a generic OpenAI-compatible HTTP interface.



Configuration:



```text

AI_BASE_URL

AI_API_KEY

AI_MODEL

```



This allows:



* local Ollama

* hosted OpenAI-compatible provider

* another compatible endpoint



For local/offline AI experimentation, support Ollama through the same configurable interface.



Do not require AI for project execution.
